# A quiet corner at Moonlit Lake

**Status:** implemented

An optional project beside the path to the fishing dock: clear three branch piles, choose a bench facing the lake or sunset, then invite Oliver to spend time at the dock. No materials, deadline or story gate are required; the invitation requires his fishing rod.

Each pile takes 15 in-game minutes and two work swings. Placing the bench takes 15 minutes. The bench is usable for free sitting. Flowers appear immediately, and two circling birds return the next morning at 6 AM. The choice is permanent for this small project.

Oliver walks to the dock and stays through schedule changes. Catching a fish within 9 metres of him after he arrives grants 20 friendship points once per in-game day; ordinary fishing time costs still apply. He comments on the shared catch and remembers it in conversation. End the visit at the sign, or walk more than 55 metres from the corner. Reloading ends visits but preserves permanent progress and the reward cooldown.

History: 2026-11-25 — built the first optional restoration and shared-activity slice.
